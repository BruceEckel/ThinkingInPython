GETTING THINKING IN PYTHON ONTO YOUR KINDLE
===========================================

Which file
----------

The release has two EPUBs. Pick the one for what you read on:

  ThinkingInPython-eink.epub   Kindle e-readers: Paperwhite, Basic,
                               Oasis, Scribe. Code is marked with bold
                               and italic, since these screens show no
                               color.

  ThinkingInPython-color.epub  Kindle Colorsoft, and the Kindle app on
                               a phone, tablet, or computer. Code is
                               color-highlighted.

The steps below say "the EPUB"; use whichever one you picked. Each
way sends the file to your Amazon account, which delivers it to your
Kindle over Wi-Fi. A Kindle cannot open an EPUB copied over a USB
cable; see Way 5 for that case.

(The book is also online at https://thinkinginpython.com, which suits
a phone, tablet, or computer. A Kindle's browser is not up to reading
it there; on a Kindle, send the file.)

The book arrives in the Kindle's library within a few minutes. If it
does not, make sure the Kindle is on Wi-Fi, then sync: swipe down
from the top of the screen and tap Sync, or open Settings and choose
Sync My Kindle. In the library, the Docs filter shows sent files.


Way 1: The Send to Kindle web page (simplest)
---------------------------------------------

Works from any computer with a web browser. Nothing to install.

  1. Download the EPUB to your computer.
  2. Open https://www.amazon.com/sendtokindle in your browser and
     sign in with the Amazon account your Kindle is registered to.
  3. Drag the EPUB onto the page, or click "Select files" and
     choose it.
  4. Check the box for the Kindle you want it delivered to (you can
     pick several).
  5. Click Send.

That is all. The page accepts files up to 200 MB; this book is
about 2 MB.


Way 2: The Send to Kindle app for Windows or macOS
--------------------------------------------------

The same service as Way 1, as a desktop app. Worth installing if you
will send files more than once.

  1. Download the app from https://www.amazon.com/sendtokindle
     (there is a Windows version and a macOS version). Install it
     and sign in with your Amazon account.
  2. Download the EPUB.
  3. Either
       - drag the EPUB onto the app's window, or
       - on Windows, right-click the EPUB in File Explorer and choose
         "Send to Kindle"; on macOS, right-click it in Finder and
         choose Share, then "Send to Kindle".
  4. In the dialog that opens, check the Kindle you want, and click
     Send.


Way 3: From a phone or tablet with the Kindle app
-------------------------------------------------

If the EPUB is on your phone (downloaded in the browser, or attached
to an email), the Kindle app can send it to your Kindle e-reader too.

  1. Install the Kindle app from the App Store or Google Play and
     sign in with your Amazon account.
  2. Open the EPUB where it is: in the Files app or Mail on an
     iPhone or iPad, or in Files, Downloads, or Gmail on Android.
  3. Tap the Share button.
  4. Choose Kindle from the list. On an iPhone or iPad the first
     time, you may need to scroll the list to the end, tap More, and
     turn Kindle on.
  5. Choose the Kindle you want it delivered to and tap Send.


Way 4: Email the file to your Kindle
------------------------------------

Every Kindle has its own email address ending in @kindle.com. Mail
sent there is delivered to the device, as long as the sending address
is on your approved list. Two one-time setup steps, then it is just
an email.

  Setup, once:

  1. On amazon.com, open Account & Lists, then "Manage Your Content
     and Devices" (https://www.amazon.com/mycd).
  2. Open the Preferences tab and expand "Personal Document
     Settings".
  3. Under "Send-to-Kindle E-Mail Settings", note the address of
     your Kindle (something like yourname_1234@kindle.com). You can
     click Edit to change it to something easier to remember.
  4. Under "Approved Personal Document E-mail List", click "Add a new
     approved e-mail address" and add the email address you will
     send from. Amazon discards mail from any address not on this
     list.

  Sending:

  5. Write an email from that approved address to your Kindle's
     address. The subject and body can be anything or empty.
  6. Attach the EPUB and send it.

Email attachments are limited to 50 MB, far above this book's size.


Way 5: USB cable, with a conversion (most involved)
---------------------------------------------------

A Kindle does not read EPUB files from its own storage, so copying
the EPUB over a USB cable does nothing useful. If you want to avoid
Amazon's delivery service (no Wi-Fi, or a Kindle you keep off your
account), convert the book to a Kindle format first with Calibre, a
free program for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

  1. Download and install Calibre from https://calibre-ebook.com.
  2. Open Calibre and click "Add books"; choose the EPUB.
  3. Connect the Kindle to the computer with its USB cable. Calibre
     detects it and a "Device" button appears in the toolbar.
  4. Select the book in Calibre's list and click "Send to device".
     Calibre converts the EPUB to a format the Kindle reads (AZW3) as
     part of sending it; if it asks whether to convert, say yes.
  5. Wait for Calibre's job counter (bottom right) to reach zero,
     then click the Device button's menu and choose "Eject this
     device", or eject it from the operating system, before unplugging.

The book appears in the Kindle's library the next time the screen
refreshes.

If you would rather convert without sending: select the book, click
"Convert books", set the output format to AZW3, click OK, then use
"Open containing folder" (right-click the book) to find the .azw3
file and copy it into the "documents" folder on the Kindle's USB
drive.


If it does not show up
----------------------

  - Wait a few minutes, then sync (swipe down, tap Sync).
  - Confirm the Kindle is signed in to the same Amazon account you
    sent from, and that it has Wi-Fi.
  - For Way 4, confirm the address you sent from is on the approved
    list, and that you sent to the Kindle's address, not your own.
  - In the library, tap the filter and choose Docs; sent files can be
    sorted separately from purchased books.
  - Amazon's own help for all of these is at
    https://www.amazon.com/sendtokindle.
