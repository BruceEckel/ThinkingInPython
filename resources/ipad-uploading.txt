READING THINKING IN PYTHON ON AN IPAD
=====================================

Way 1: Read it online at ThinkingInPython.com (simplest)
--------------------------------------------------------

Nothing to download. In Safari, open

    https://thinkinginpython.com

The table of contents is on the front page, each chapter is one page
with Previous/Next links at the end, and there is a search box. It is
always the current text, built from the same source as the release
files. For an icon on the Home Screen that opens the book like an app,
tap the Share button and choose "Add to Home Screen".

The files below are for reading offline, or for keeping a copy of a
particular release.


Which file
----------

The release has two EPUBs and a PDF. On an iPad, use:

  ThinkingInPython-color.epub  The one to pick. Code is
                               color-highlighted, and an EPUB reflows
                               to fit the screen, so you can change
                               the type size and the text adjusts.

  ThinkingInPython.pdf         Also readable on an iPad (Apple Books
                               opens it), but its pages are fixed, so
                               small type stays small. Prefer the EPUB.

  ThinkingInPython-eink.epub   For e-ink Kindles. It works on an iPad
                               but shows code in bold and italic
                               instead of color; there is no reason to
                               choose it here.

The steps below say "the EPUB"; that means ThinkingInPython-color.epub.


Way 2: Open it in Apple Books, straight from Safari
---------------------------------------------------

Apple Books comes with every iPad and reads EPUBs. Nothing to install.

  1. In Safari on the iPad, open the book's releases page and tap the
     EPUB. Safari asks whether to download it; tap Download.
  2. Tap the Downloads button (the circled arrow at the top right of
     Safari) and tap the EPUB in the list.
     If Safari shows a preview instead, tap the Share button (the
     square with an arrow) and choose Books.
  3. Books opens with the book added to your library.

If Books does not open it, open the Files app, go to Downloads, tap
the EPUB, tap the Share button, and choose Books.

Books keeps the book in your library and, with iCloud, on your other
Apple devices too.


Way 3: From a computer
----------------------

If you downloaded the EPUB on a computer, get it to the iPad with
whichever of these you already use, then open it in Books.

  AirDrop, from a Mac:

  1. In Finder, right-click the EPUB, choose Share, then AirDrop.
  2. Pick your iPad. On the iPad, accept it; it opens in Books.

  iCloud Drive, from a Mac or a PC with iCloud installed:

  1. Copy the EPUB into your iCloud Drive folder.
  2. On the iPad, open the Files app, go to iCloud Drive, tap the
     EPUB, then tap Share and choose Books.

  Email to yourself, from anything:

  1. Attach the EPUB to an email and send it to an address you read
     on the iPad.
  2. On the iPad, open the email, tap the attachment, tap Share, and
     choose Books.

  A cable, from a Mac:

  1. Connect the iPad and open it in Finder's sidebar.
  2. Open the Files tab, and drag the EPUB onto Books in the list.
     It appears in the Books app on the iPad.


Way 4: The Kindle app, so it is in your Amazon library
------------------------------------------------------

Choose this if you also read on a Kindle e-reader and want your
place, highlights, and notes to sync between the two, or you simply
prefer the Kindle app. The book goes into your Amazon library and
downloads to every Kindle app and device on your account.

  1. Install the Kindle app from the App Store and sign in with your
     Amazon account.
  2. Get the EPUB onto the iPad by Way 2 or Way 3 (it does not have
     to be opened in Books; Downloads in the Files app is enough).
  3. In the Files app, tap the EPUB, then tap the Share button.
  4. Choose Kindle from the row of apps. The first time, you may need
     to scroll that row to the end, tap More, and turn Kindle on.
  5. The Kindle app shows a Send to Kindle screen. Tap Send.
  6. Open the Kindle app. The book appears in your library within a
     few minutes; pull down on the library to refresh. Tap it to
     download.

Two other routes to the same place: the Send to Kindle web page
(https://www.amazon.com/sendtokindle, from any browser, drag the file
in), or email the EPUB to your Kindle address. Both are described
step by step in kindle-uploading.txt, which ships alongside this file.


Way 5: Another reader app
-------------------------

Any reader app that accepts EPUB works the same way as Books: get the
file onto the iPad (Way 2 or Way 3), tap it in the Files app, tap
Share, and choose the app. If the app is not in the row, scroll to the
end and tap More; apps that can open an EPUB are listed there. Some
apps also have their own Import or "+" button that browses the Files
app directly.


If it does not open
-------------------

  - Make sure you downloaded the file rather than viewing it: in
    Safari, the Downloads button lists what was saved.
  - Tap the file in the Files app and use Share; Books and Kindle
    both appear there, and Books is also offered as "Open in Books"
    at the top of the sheet on many iPad versions.
  - If Books says the file is invalid, download it again; a
    download interrupted partway through leaves a file Books cannot
    read.
  - For the Kindle app, confirm it is signed in to the same Amazon
    account that received the file, and refresh the library.
