READING THINKING IN PYTHON ON AN ANDROID PHONE OR TABLET
========================================================

Way 1: Read it online at ThinkingInPython.com (simplest)
--------------------------------------------------------

Nothing to download. In Chrome, open

    https://thinkinginpython.com

The table of contents is on the front page, each chapter is one page
with Previous/Next links at the end, and there is a search box. It is
always the current text, built from the same source as the release
files. For an icon on the home screen that opens the book like an
app, tap Chrome's menu (the three dots) and choose "Add to Home
screen".

The files below are for reading offline, or for keeping a copy of a
particular release.


Which file
----------

The release has two EPUBs and a PDF. On Android, use:

  ThinkingInPython-color.epub  The one to pick. Code is
                               color-highlighted, and an EPUB reflows
                               to fit the screen, so you can change
                               the type size and the text adjusts.

  ThinkingInPython.pdf         Also readable (Google Drive, Chrome, and
                               most file managers open it), but its
                               pages are fixed, so small type stays
                               small. Prefer the EPUB.

  ThinkingInPython-eink.epub   For e-ink Kindles. It works on Android
                               but shows code in bold and italic
                               instead of color; no reason to choose
                               it here.

The steps below say "the EPUB"; that means ThinkingInPython-color.epub.

Every way starts the same: in Chrome, open the book's releases page,
tap the EPUB, and it downloads. Chrome shows a "Download complete"
notice; the file is in the Downloads folder, which the Files app (or
"Files by Google", or your maker's file manager) lists.


Way 2: Google Play Books
------------------------

Play Books comes on most Android devices and reads EPUBs. The book
goes into your Google account's library, so it also shows up on your
other devices and at play.google.com/books.

  1. Download the EPUB (above).
  2. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, and tap the EPUB. If Play
     Books is the only app that reads EPUB, it opens right away.
     If Android asks which app to use, choose Play Books.
  3. Play Books opens the book and adds it to your library under
     "Uploads".

If tapping the file offers nothing suitable, upload from inside Play
Books instead: open Play Books, tap your profile picture, then
"Play Books settings", and turn on "Enable PDF uploading" (it covers
EPUB too). Then tap Library, then "Upload files", and choose the EPUB.

From a computer, the same library accepts uploads at
https://play.google.com/books/uploads.


Way 3: The Kindle app, so it is in your Amazon library
------------------------------------------------------

Choose this if you also read on a Kindle e-reader and want your
place, highlights, and notes to sync between the two, or you prefer
the Kindle app. The book goes into your Amazon library and downloads
to every Kindle app and device on your account.

  1. Install the Kindle app from Google Play and sign in with your
     Amazon account.
  2. Download the EPUB (above).
  3. In the Files app, long-press the EPUB and tap Share (or open it
     and use the Share button).
  4. Choose Kindle (it may be labeled "Send to Kindle") from the
     list of apps.
  5. On the Send to Kindle screen, tap Send.
  6. Open the Kindle app. The book appears in the library within a
     few minutes; pull down on the library to refresh. Tap it to
     download.

Two other routes to the same place: the Send to Kindle web page
(https://www.amazon.com/sendtokindle, from any browser, drag the file
in), or email the EPUB to your Kindle address. Both are described
step by step in kindle-uploading.txt, which ships alongside this file.


Way 4: Another reader app
-------------------------

Any reader that accepts EPUB works: for example Moon+ Reader, Lithium,
or ReadEra from Google Play. Install it, then either tap the EPUB in
the Files app and choose the reader when asked, or use the reader's
own Import or "+" button, which browses your folders directly.
Most of them also find the file on their own if you open them and
look under Downloads or "Scan".


If it does not open
-------------------

  - Confirm the download finished: Chrome's download list (the menu,
    then Downloads) shows it, and the Files app lists it under
    Downloads. Tap the file from there rather than from the Chrome
    notice, which can expire.
  - If a tap opens the wrong app, long-press the file, choose
    "Open with", and pick the reader; Android remembers a default and
    this changes it.
  - If Play Books refuses the file, enable uploading in its settings
    (Way 2) and use "Upload files".
  - If a reader says the file is damaged, download it again; an
    interrupted download leaves a file no reader can open.
  - For the Kindle app, confirm it is signed in to the same Amazon
    account that received the file, and refresh the library.
