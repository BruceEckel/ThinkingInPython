## Notes

- Solution 6's prose paraphrases the stdlib `Generator.send` signature
  as `send(self, value: _SendT_contra) -> _YieldT_co`. Running
  `ty check` on the stripped-`type: ignore` file shows typeshed's
  actual signature is `send(self, value: _SendT_contra, /) -> _YieldT_co`
  (positional-only marker `/`). Cosmetic only — doesn't affect the
  exercise's teaching point (that `send()`'s parameter type is the
  `SendType`) — so I'm not counting it as a finding, just flagging it
  for a human to decide whether to tighten the quote.
- Exercise 2 asks the reader to "give your driver fewer answers than
  questions and say what it returns." The solution's better driver
  (`drive_in_order`) actually *raises* `StopIteration` rather than
  returning anything in that case (`drive_naive`, which does return,
  is presented as the wrong way). The solution's prose addresses this
  explicitly and well, so I'm not filing it as a defect, but the
  exercise wording assumes a return where the taught answer is "it
  doesn't return, and here's why that's the right choice." A human may
  want to loosen "say what it returns" to "say what happens."
- No "previous chapter" / "previous section" phrasing appears in this
  chapter's exercises, so the chapter-46 split (chapter 45 was split
  out of it) has no dangling references here to check.
